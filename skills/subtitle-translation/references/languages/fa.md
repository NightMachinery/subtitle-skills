# Persian language advice

Use concise, faithful, idiomatic Persian. Preserve technical distinctions and do not invent explanations. When translating from Persian, preserve these distinctions in the chosen target language.

For CBT material, use this glossary when the corresponding concept occurs:

- CBT: درمان شناختی رفتاری
- thought record: ثبت افکار
- cognitive restructuring: بازسازی شناختی
- core belief: باور بنیادی
- behavioral activation: فعال‌سازی رفتاری
- contingency management: مدیریت اقتضایی
- rumination: نشخوار فکری
- assertiveness: جرئت‌مندی

Keep ZWNJ where Persian orthography requires it, such as فعال‌سازی. Preserve legitimate punctuation and separate dialogue lines beginning `- ` for two speakers. Actual UTF-8 text needs no manual bidi wrapping. Do not add hidden directional controls. Review readability, line breaks, and terminology in the target subtitle player.

The clinical use of مدیریت اقتضایی is supported by the [Persian DHCS program definition](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/fa/recovery-incentives-program-californias-contingency-management-benefit/). Keep terminology consistent within an episode; do not translate this behavioral reinforcement term as addiction/dependence management.
